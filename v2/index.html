How it works

Three moves. Only one of them is yours.

  1. 01

    You describe it

    In your own words, in any of sixteen languages, in the assistant you already have open.

  2. 02

    BugIt does the write up

    Title, steps, expected and actual, severity and platform, in your team's language and house style.

  3. 03

    You type FILE IT

    Then it goes to your tracker with your own token. Not a moment before.

    Commercial Transactions

    Do I need GitHub Copilot?

    No. BugIt runs in any assistant that can run a command: Copilot Chat, the Claude extension in VS Code, or a plain terminal. It can also run standalone with your own OpenAI or Anthropic key.

    Does Taskivator see my tickets?

    No. Your tickets, specs and tokens do not pass through our servers. Only license and update data does, and every field is listed in the privacy notice.

    What do I need to run it?

    An AI assistant that can run a command, Python 3.10 to 3.14, and the tracker you already use. Windows 11 is fully supported; macOS and Linux are in preview.

    What happens after a year?

    Your files and settings stay. Filing needs an active license, and nothing renews unless you buy again.
