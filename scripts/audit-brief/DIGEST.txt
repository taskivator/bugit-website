DIGESTED UP TO: 2026-10-04
This is the FIRST review dedicated to bugit.dev. Until now the site was reviewed only in passing,
as part of the BugIt agent's external audits (bugit.dev was in their scope signed out, by URL),
and by our own internal reviews. What those found and what we changed, so you do not report it
again:

  * Claims. Two claims were found to be false and removed everywhere on 2026-10-04, from the site,
    the docs, all eleven languages and the films: that BugIt "compares your whole message" before
    filing, and that data "never leaves your machine". Redaction is described as best effort.
    These are in contract 2.1 so that any copy that survived is reported.
  * Pricing. The introductory price was retired on 2026-10-03; one price per plan since. Any
    remaining introductory, launch or "one-time" wording is a finding (contract 2.2).
  * The redesign. The pages visitors see were replaced by the 2026 redesign (v2/) on 2026-10-04.
    About forty guards that checked the old single page were retired the same day and the copy
    guards now read the published copy itself. A guard that still tests the old page instead of
    the new one, or a contract the old page kept that the new one dropped, is worth reporting.
  * Phone poster. On a phone the film's caption overlapped the play button (fixed 2026-10-04: the
    caption sits under the picture below 640px). The play button was redesigned the same day.
  * Delivery. On 2026-09-23 a deploy built from the wrong branch removed two features from
    production. Since then every deploy refuses a build that would remove something production
    serves, and verifies every file on the edge byte for byte afterwards.

STATED LIMITS, not findings unless you show they are worse than described:
  * Redaction in the product is pattern based (the site says so).
  * The Google Ads tag is present on the page; it runs with every signal denied until consent.
  * The CSP permits Cloudflare's cookieless Web Analytics beacon; the page source does not load it.
