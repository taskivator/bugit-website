1.1 Severity: by what a visitor or a customer loses
  CRITICAL  A visitor's data or the owner's private identity is exposed, advertising or analytics
            data is collected without consent, or a page tells people something false that could
            cost them money (a wrong price, licence term or refund term).
  HIGH      A false or unprovable claim about what BugIt does; a legal page that is wrong or
            unreachable; a flow a visitor needs (reading the product, pricing, the trial steps,
            the docs, reaching support) broken on a common browser or phone.
  MEDIUM    Something works but misleads, is inaccessible to keyboard, screen reader, zoom or
            reduced motion users, or breaks at some screen widths or in some language.
  LOW       Polish: small layout or wording issues that do not mislead.
  INFO      Observations and suggestions.
  Between two levels, take the higher and say why.

2. OUR CONTRACTS: WHAT THE SITE PROMISES (please test these as a specification)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2.1 What BugIt is. A QA agent that runs inside the AI assistant the customer already uses
      (GitHub Copilot Chat, Claude in VS Code, or a terminal). It drafts a bug report from what
      the user describes, runs its checks, and files to the user's tracker only after the user has
      read the draft and typed FILE IT. Eleven trackers. Every claim on the site must be true of
      the product and checkable. Three claims are known to be FALSE and must appear nowhere, in
      any language, on any page, in any prepared Guide answer or alt text:
        * that BugIt compares or reads "your whole message" (the confirmation is checked on the
          command line the assistant writes, not by reading the chat);
        * that anything "never leaves" or "stays on" the user's machine (the conversation goes to
          the AI model the user chose; filing goes to their tracker);
        * that personal data is guaranteed removed (redaction is pattern based and best effort).
  2.2 Prices. Solo $59.99 and Team $249.99, each a 1-year license with no subscription and no
      automatic renewal. The purchase is never described as one-time, lifetime or a one-off.
      There is no introductory, launch or discounted price. The pricing section, the docs, the
      Guide's answers, every language, and https://portal.bugit.dev/pricing must all agree.
  2.3 The free trial. A limited number of free Solo licenses for testers who give honest
      feedback, requested in the portal (Support, Request a free trial). The site never states how
      many trials or testers there are.
  2.4 Consent and tracking. The Google Ads tag runs under Consent Mode v2 with every signal
      DENIED by default; nothing advertising related is stored or sent until the visitor accepts.
      The decision is kept in one cookie, bugit_consent, shared with portal.bugit.dev, and can be
      changed from "Cookie preferences" in the footer. No other third party script is loaded
      by the page source. The CSP also permits Cloudflare's cookieless Web Analytics beacon, which
      Cloudflare can add at the edge; if you see it, it must set no cookie. Any cookie, storage or
      third party request that happens BEFORE consent beyond these, or any advertising request
      after a decline, is a finding.
  2.5 Languages. English plus Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean,
      Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Chinese. Every visible string, alt text and aria label is
      translated; Arabic is right to left and must lay out as such; a language choice persists.
      Spanish and Italian use informal address; Chinese uses informal address.
  2.6 The Guide (Ask). It answers from prepared content served by bugit.dev. An answer that
      contradicts the product pages or the docs, or makes a claim from 2.1 to 2.3 the site does
      not, is a finding. It must be usable by keyboard and screen reader.
  2.7 The owner's private identity. The owner's home address and personal phone number are never
      published anywhere on the site.
  2.8 No en or em dashes in visitor facing copy, in any language.
  2.9 Security and delivery: the Content-Security-Policy in _headers (default-src 'self'; scripts
      from 'self', Cloudflare Web Analytics and the Google tag hosts only; frame-src only
      youtube-nocookie.com; form-action 'self' and portal.bugit.dev; no inline script), plus HSTS,
      nosniff, X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN, a referrer policy and a permissions policy, on every
      page, asset and 404. https://bugit.dev/verify.json
      publishes the digest of the currently published BugIt package. Repository files that are
      not part of the site (scripts, tests, configuration) must answer 404.
  2.10 Videos. The introduction is a self hosted poster that LINKS to https://www.youtube.com/
       watch?v=nlLLNSYlzqo (nothing is embedded on page load); the channel is @BugItByTaskivator.
       The poster's play button must work by keyboard and stay still under reduced motion.
  2.11 Accessibility and layout: every page works by keyboard and at 200% zoom; with reduced
       motion the demos stop; nothing overflows sideways from 320px to wide desktop; no text sits
       on other text; forced colours keep controls visible.
  2.12 SEO: one canonical URL per page, a title and a description; the sitemap lists only real
       pages.
